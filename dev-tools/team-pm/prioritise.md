# Prioritisation and roadmap

## Prioritising

Rate each candidate:

- **Impact**: how much it moves the success signals or unblocks users.
- **Confidence**: how sure we are. Evidence or guess?
- **Effort**: rough size.
- **Urgency**: deadlines, customer commitments, dependencies.

Show a sorted table. Recommend what to do first and say what drops.

- Say no by default. Every yes needs a reason.
- Finish before starting something new.
- High impact and cheap goes first. Then whatever reduces the biggest risk.

## Roadmap check

When I ask "are we working on the right things?":

1. Pull the current projects, milestones and in-progress tickets from the tracker.
2. Compare them with the roadmap and priorities.
3. Flag:
   - work not tied to a priority
   - priorities nobody is working on
   - blocked or stale tickets
   - scope creep (tickets added after the project started)
4. Recommend what to stop, start and continue.

Output: a status table (milestone, progress, risk), the flags, then the recommendation.
