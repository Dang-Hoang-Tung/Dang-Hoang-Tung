description: Guides on designing UI/UX, and developing prototypes for new features and products.
---

Here are the steps to follow when designing a new feature or product:

1. Understand the problem and context
2. Identify the relevant product areas, pages, and components
3. Identify the relevant user flows and interactions
4. Create mockups and prototypes for the new feature or product based on the relevant elements, and leveraging the design system
   a.
   b. If the design system does not have the relevant elements, you can create one-off mocks, they may be added to the DS later.

Approach:

- Use our codebase to access the components and pages
- Mock the provider or endpoints to simulate the data and interactions
- Spin up a local browser for me to test the design
